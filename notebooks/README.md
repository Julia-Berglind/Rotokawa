# Notebooks

| Notebook | What it does | Main outputs |
|---|---|---|
| `A0_Maps` | Map of the existing stations (Mercury at Rotokawa and Ngā Tamariki, GeoNet) on a hillshade with lakes and rivers. | `figures/existing_stations_map.png`, `.pdf` |
| `01_velocity_model` | Builds the 3D Vp and Vs model from the known geology: layers, faults (Calibugan et al. 2022 structure) and greywacke basement. No BDT. | `velocity_model.npz` |
| `02_designs` | Station layouts in two networks. Trillium network: 15 Trilliums plus the existing stations (Mercury from `data/RTK_NTM_Station_Coordinates.dat`, and GeoNet), all recording for the whole deployment. Nodal network (30 nodes): static designs (dense grid, wide grid, spiral) and phased designs, which start with a wide reconnaissance grid over the MT target area (4 km around the drill site), then move to a denser grid on the target picked from phase 1, in 3 patches (rolling or interleaved). Also writes the station bank phase 2 can be placed on, and maps the current stations and every design. | `stations.csv`, `designs.json`, `figures/array_designs.png` |
| `03_travel_times` | P and S travel times from every station, including the phase 2 bank, to every model cell (eikonal), plus 1D tables. Slow the first time; existing tables are skipped, so delete `data/tt/` after rerunning 02. | `tt/` |
| `mi_common.py` | Matrix imaging shared by 04 and 05: a dome-shaped BDT zone (Vp +5 %) as Born scatterers, reflection matrices between stations that record together, confocal imaging with the 3D travel times, picks and scores, phase 2 laid out around the phase 1 pick with 02's rules, and the correlation-noise model. | — |
| `04_matrix_imaging` | For every design: simulate the recordings over the dome, image them and compare with the truth, with fully converged (noise-free) correlations. Phased designs run as in the field: image phase 1, pick the BDT high, place phase 2 there. Shows the recovered images (sections and picked-top maps) and scores the crest depth, the phase 1 pick and coverage of the BDT top in the MT target area. | `results_05_mi.csv`, `mi_images_05/` |
| `05_noise` | 04 with correlation noise that shrinks with the time each station pair records together (√days), at one or more noise levels (`NSR`). The phase 1 pick uses only data recorded by the end of phase 1. Shows the noisy images next to 04's noise-free ones. | `results_06_noise.csv`, `mi_images_06/` |
| `matrix_imaging_pipeline.py`, `geonet_matrix_imaging.py` | First-pass passive matrix imaging with real data: one hour of vertical noise from five GeoNet stations (WPRZ, ARAZ, THQ2, ALRZ, THSZ), correlated and focused with Rayleigh travel times through the velocity model of 01 (2D fast marching through a phase-velocity map estimated from each Vs column). Run `python geonet_matrix_imaging.py` from `notebooks/`. | `geonet_matrix_imaging/` (waveforms, inventory, `mi_results.npz`, figures) |
